BEHIND THE RANKINGS

Signal over noise.

We rank recurring content patterns, not individual viral videos. Every live issue is a sample-based research report, not an official cross-platform leaderboard.

01 / COLLECT
◎

Find the videos.

Collect the previous month’s videos from a licensed feed across niches, filtering publication dates and short durations.

02 / CLASSIFY
▧

Decode the format.

Map provider-approved labels into a transparent 50-format taxonomy; metadata AI tagging is opt-in and rights-dependent.

03 / RANK
↗

Score the signal.

Combine views per day, engagement rate, independent creators, and cross-niche reach. Open the source examples to check the evidence.

04 / REFRESH
↻

Repeat monthly.

After setup, GitHub Actions produces a new issue on the second of every month, archives it, and republishes the website.

Read this before using rankings. Rankings depend on the data rights, coverage, and accuracy of the connected feed. A video title cannot reliably prove its editing style, and views per day are not measured view growth. The report is an independent estimate, not official platform analytics. Ordinary YouTube API access does not authorize derivative ranking metrics.
